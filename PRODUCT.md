# WhiteboardX

## Register

product

## Users

Design and creative teams — designers, product people, and collaborators doing real-time visual ideation, wireframing, and brainstorming. They're in flow, switching between drawing, discussing, and iterating. Speed and ease matter more than feature depth.

## Product Purpose

A real-time collaborative whiteboard that lets creative teams think together visually. No setup friction, no save anxiety, no "who drew what" confusion. Success looks like: a team opens it mid-meeting, starts sketching, and the tool disappears into the conversation.

## Brand Personality

Playful, creative, energetic. Not childish — the energy comes through in color, motion, and the feeling of moving fast. Voice is direct and warm. This is the tool for the part of the meeting where ideas take shape.

## Anti-references

- **Overly complex / cluttered tools** — no busy toolbars, overwhelming feature surfaces, or dense UIs that bury the canvas
- **Generic SaaS grayscale** — the default neutral-slate corporate look that says "admin panel," not "creative tool"
- **Disconnected brand surfaces** — the landing page and the app should feel like the same product (currently they don't)

## Design Principles

1. **The tool disappears** — earned familiarity. Users are here to draw, not to learn another interface. Standard patterns, consistent affordances, nothing cute in the way of the task.
2. **Playful is not decoration** — energy lives in color, motion on state change, and crafted details. Not in gratuitous animations or gradient text.
3. **One identity** — the landing page and app UI should feel cohesive. Not the same layout, but they should share DNA: color, type, spirit.
4. **Color as personality** — a creative tool can't be gray. Brand color carries the energy, used in proportion (Restrained for the product surface, stronger for brand moments).
5. **Craft without weight** — every component should feel considered without feeling busy. Reduce complexity, not quality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast against backgrounds. All interactive elements keyboard-accessible. Reduced motion respected. Color not the only indicator of state.
